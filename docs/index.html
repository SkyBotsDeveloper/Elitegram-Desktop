THE TOOLKIT

More control. Less clutter.

Useful additions, without turning the familiar Desktop experience inside out.

◌

Ghost Mode

Per-account controls for presence, typing, read status, and story views, with Seen on Reply where applicable. Not an anonymity guarantee.

▤

Keep Deleted Messages

Keep eligible ordinary private-cloud messages in a separate local, view-only archive after normal Telegram deletion.

⊞

Up to 100 account slots

Architecture for 100 slots. Five accounts were manually exercised; 100 simultaneous real accounts were not.

◎

Data-center details

See useful Telegram data-center information within the app.

♫

Call and voice tools

Selected Audio, Device Audio, Voice Effects, and No Mic Blink for compatible call conditions and Windows audio devices.

▣

Native Desktop feel

Elitegram branding, an isolated profile, and a compact Chats sidebar card within Telegram Desktop’s familiar layout.

CALLS & VOICE

Put your audio
where you want it.

Use a selected local audio file, route device audio, or choose a voice effect in supported calls. No Mic Blink adds a separate call control. The result still depends on your Windows devices and Telegram call state.

PRIVACY & TRANSPARENCY

Inspectable by design.
Honest about limits.

Elitegram is open source and communicates with Telegram infrastructure as a Telegram client. It stores normal session data locally. Eligible deleted content may remain in a local archive if you enable Keep Deleted Messages.

Protected, view-once, self-destructing, and secret-chat ephemeral content are not advertised as retainable. Open source improves inspectability; it is not proof of safety.
