🚀 ELITEGRAM DESKTOP IS HERE! 💻

Elitegram is now available for Windows — a native Desktop experience built on Telegram Desktop.

✨ Highlights
• Ghost Mode and privacy controls
• Keep Deleted Messages for eligible private chats
• Up to 100 account slots
• Selected Audio and Device Audio
• Voice Effects and No Mic Blink
• Data-center information
• A clean Elitegram Desktop interface

🔓 Open source, so you can inspect the implementation. Privacy controls are not an anonymity guarantee, and protected or self-destructing content is not covered by Keep Deleted Messages.
